### **Independent Codicological Study Resolves Century-Old Voynich Manuscript Mystery via Venetian Trade Routes & Mechanical Volvelles**

**YALE BEINECKE MS 408 OPEN-ACCESS DEPOSITION ANNOUNCED ON OSF AND INTERACTIVE DIGITAL ATLAS**

**NEW HAVEN, CT / VENICE, ITALY — SEPTEMBER 2026** — Independent codicological researcher **Tammy Lou Casey** today announced the formal academic deposition of ***The Mechanical Voynich: Complete Codicological, Botanical, and Venetian Trade Concordance***, an 18-volume monograph series establishing the physical cipher mechanics, paleobotanical species identification, and Venetian maritime trade origins of Yale University's Beinecke Library MS 408 (the Voynich Manuscript).

The complete dataset, 18 research monographs, and high-resolution interactive concordance atlas have been deposited in the **Open Science Framework (OSF)** under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC-BY-NC 4.0) at **[osf.io/5ghjf](https://osf.io/5ghjf)**.

---

### **Key Findings & Empirical Breakthroughs**

1. **Mechanical Cipher Rotas & Volvelles**:
   Demonstrates that the underlying text structure across all 102 folios was generated using physical grid volvelles and rotatable cipher wheels rather than natural language or synthetic jargon, explaining the observed statistical entropy and prefix/suffix character repetition.

2. **100% Paleobotanical Identification & Venetian Maritime Trade**:
   Resolves the herbal section plant specimens by matching them to 15th-century Renaissance Mediterranean flora and import species cataloged along Venetian galley convoy shipping routes (*Muda di Barbaria* to North Africa and *Muda di Fiandra* to Northern Europe).

3. **Interactive High-Resolution IIIF Concordance Atlas**:
   Maintains a public digital concordance atlas at **[voynich.ozark-oracle.com](https://voynich.ozark-oracle.com)** featuring vector overlays, line-by-line word-spotting, and 1:1 integration with Yale University Library IIIF scans.
