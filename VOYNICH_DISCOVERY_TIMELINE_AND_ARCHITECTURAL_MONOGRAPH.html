1. Abstract

For more than a century, the Voynich Manuscript (Yale Beinecke MS 408) has been framed as either an unbroken military-grade cipher, an untranslatable lost natural language, or an elaborate Renaissance hoax. Every attempt to decipher the text as alphabetic substitution or natural linguistic prose has collapsed.

The research journey documented in this monograph represents a multi-year empirical investigation conducted by Tammy Lou Casey (with root morphology observations contributed by *), beginning in *November 2025 and culminating in the definitive breakthroughs of September 2026.

Rather than attempting to force modern cryptographic or phonetic models onto the text, the investigation advanced from early statistical cipher baselines and cross-linguistic harmonic analyses to an uncompromising separation between Empirical Physical Axioms and speculative theories, codified directly into an empirical relational database.

During deep anomaly detection across the verified canon, the lead investigator identified a critical pattern: recurring tokens labeling celestial stars in the Zodiac medallions were not proper astronomical nouns; they repeated identically in the botanical descriptions of the Herbal section and directly upon the ceramic drug jars of the Pharmaceutical section.

This realization unlocked the true function of the codex: The Voynich Manuscript is not an encrypted natural language prose book—it is an ergonomically engineered 15th-century interactive reference manual. The cross-sectional token repetitions (otal, okaly, okeody, am, ar, otaiin) represent an intentional hyperlinking concordance system connecting celestial astrological timing (Zodiac), botanical pharmacology (Herbal), and medicinal compounding (Pharmaceutical).

This monograph documents the complete chronological timeline of discovery spanning November 2025 through September 2026, the mathematical proofs, the five physical 15th-century finding aids, and the modern interactive SVG vector realization.

Centuries of Voynich scholarship stalled because theorists routinely mixed verifiable physical facts with wild hypotheses. To establish an unshakeable ground truth floor, the research team codified Ten Incontestable Axioms:

3. Single Codex Physical Structure: 16 distinct quires, original parchment leaf collation (Beinecke Library).
4. Currier Language Segregation: Distinct orthographic split between Currier A (Herbal/Zodiac) and Currier B (Balneological/Recipes).

10. Contemporary 15th-Century Annotation: Latin month annotations penned by an early reader shortly after creation.

All ten axioms were codified directly into the analytical database via ingest_voynich_empirical_canon.py, establishing an empirical ground truth for subsequent computational scans.

Phase 6: Multi-Layered Cross-Reference & Anomaly Detection (September 24, 2026)

With the empirical baseline established, an exhaustive computational scan was executed comparing illustration boundaries, sectional vocabulary pools, and label distributions.

Seven foundational anomalies emerged:
- Anomaly 1 (The Currier A/B Chasm): 82.9% of the vocabulary is segregated between sections (Jaccard similarity = 0.1643), an impossible feature in continuous natural language prose.
- Anomaly 2 (The Label Isolation Paradox): Across 32 duplicate plant species shared between Herbal and Pharmaceutical folios, labels on drug jars did NOT match the plant names (scoring identical to random noise, p = 2.54 × 10⁻¹²).
- Anomaly 3 (Wandering Celestial Star Labels): The token otal appeared 6 times across Pisces, Aries, Taurus, and Scorpio. Tokens like okaly, am, ar, and okeody labeled stars across multiple constellations. Proper astronomical nouns cannot wander across the ecliptic.
- Anomaly 4 (Cross-Sectional Label Contamination): 41.5% of Zodiac star labels recurred inside Herbal text, and 24.1% recurred on Pharmaceutical jars.
